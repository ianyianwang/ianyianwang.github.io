Help at home

You do not need to be a teacher. You do not need perfect English. Ten happy minutes a day makes a big difference.

Every day: 10 minutes

  1. Sounds (2 minutes). Point to sounds your child knows. Your child says them fast. Remember: pure sounds, “mmm” not “muh”.
  2. Read the school book (5 minutes). Your child reads to you. Let them read it again tomorrow. Reading a book 2 or 3 times makes them faster and more confident.
  3. Talk (3 minutes). Ask one or two questions about the story. You can ask them in your home language.
  4. Bedtime story. You read to your child, in any language. Enjoy it together!

When your child reads to you

✅ Please do

  • Sit together somewhere quiet, with the TV off.
  • Say “Fred Talk it” if your child is stuck on a word.
  • Praise effort: “Great Fred Talking!”
  • Stop while it is still fun.

❌ Try not to

  • Give the answer too quickly. Wait a few seconds first.
  • Teach letter names (“ay, bee, see”). Use the sounds.
  • Ask your child to read books that are too hard for them.
  • Worry if it is slow. Every child learns at their own speed.

Why reading matters

Reading is the key to all learning. When children read well, they do better in every subject, not only English.

📚

Reading opens every subject

Maths, Science and Humanities all need reading. A child who reads well can learn on their own.

💬

Reading builds words

Books use many words we do not say in everyday talk. Children learn these words from stories.

❤️

Reading builds confidence

Children who enjoy books feel proud and calm. They want to learn more.

Why read to your child?

Your child is learning to read by themselves at school. But it is still very important that you read to them too, every day if you can.

  • Your child hears stories that are harder than the books they can read alone.
  • They learn new words and new ideas about the world.
  • They hear how a good reader sounds: the speed, the expression, the voices.
  • It is a happy, quiet time together. Children who love stories want to read.
You can read in any language. Reading stories in Thai, Chinese or your home language is also very good for your child. Talking about the story matters more than the language.

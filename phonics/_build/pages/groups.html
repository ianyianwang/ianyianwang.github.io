Reading groups

Your child reads in a small group with children at the same level. Each group has a colour. The colour matches the books they read.

Moving groups is normal. We check every child's reading often. When your child is ready, they move up to the next colour. Every child learns at their own speed. Please do not compare your child with other children.

Ask class teachers how parents find out their child's group (reading record? Toddle? report?) and add one sentence here. Also confirm which colour groups SBS runs this year: the Canva page only had Red Ditty, Green and Orange.

The colour groups, from first to last

  1. Red Ditty

    Your child knows many Set 1 sounds and is starting to blend sounds into short words, like “c-a-t”.

    At home: say Set 1 sounds together. Play “Fred Talk”: you say “p-i-g”, your child says “pig”.

  2. Red

    Your child reads short stories with Set 1 sounds and first special friends (sh, th, ch).

    At home: read the book again. Point to the red words (like “the” and “I”) and say them together.

  3. Green

    Your child reads Set 1 sounds quickly and reads longer words, like “frog” and “stamp”.

    At home: ask your child to read with “Fred in your head”: sounds quietly, then the word out loud.

  4. Purple

    Your child starts learning Set 2 sounds (ay, ee, igh…).

    At home: find Set 2 sounds in signs, menus and labels when you are out.

  5. Pink

    Your child reads stories with more Set 2 sounds.

    At home: after reading, ask “What happened first? What happened next?”

  6. Orange

    Your child knows all Set 2 sounds and reads longer stories.

    At home: ask your child to read with a “story voice”, not a robot voice.

  7. Yellow

    Your child starts learning Set 3 sounds (new ways to write the same sound).

    At home: talk about new words. Ask “What do you think this word means?”

  8. Blue

    Your child reads stories and non-fiction with Set 3 sounds.

    At home: ask “Why do you think the character did that?”

  9. Grey

    The last group. Your child reads long stories fluently and is nearly ready to finish phonics.

    At home: choose library books together and talk about them.

Phonics lead to check the description of each group against what SBS teaches. Add the Red Ditty, Green and Orange group pages/resources from the Canva site if there are any (they were empty buttons on the Canva page).

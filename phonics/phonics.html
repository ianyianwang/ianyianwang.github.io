How phonics works

Phonics means learning the sounds that letters make, then putting the sounds together to read words.

What is Read Write Inc. Phonics?

Read Write Inc. (we say “R-W-I”) is a phonics programme made by Ruth Miskin in the UK. Many schools around the world use it. At SBS Bangkok, children have a phonics lesson every day.

Confirm which year groups have RWI lessons (e.g. Reception to Year 2?), how long each lesson is, and how often groups are checked (RWI suggests about every 6–8 weeks).

👂

1. Learn the sounds

Children learn one sound at a time, with a picture and a short rhyme to help them remember.

🔗

2. Blend the sounds

They say the sounds in a word, then push them together: c-a-t → cat.

📖

3. Read a book

They read a storybook that only uses the sounds they already know. So they can read it!

✏️

4. Write

They use the same sounds to spell and write words and sentences.

Children learn in small groups. Each group reads at the same level. Children move up to the next group when they are ready, so every child learns at the right speed. See Step 4: Reading groups.

Words your child uses in class

Your child may say these words at home. Here is what they mean.

FredA toy frog used in lessons. Fred can only say words in sounds: “c-a-t”. Children help Fred by saying the whole word: “cat!”
Fred TalkSaying a word in its sounds: d-o-g. This is how children read new words.
Fred in your headSaying the sounds quietly in your head, then reading the word out loud.
Speed SoundsThe sounds your child learns. They practise them until they can say them fast. See Step 3.
Special friendsTwo or three letters that make one sound together, like sh in “ship” or ee in “tree”.
Green wordsWords you can read with Fred Talk, like “sun” or “shop”.
Red wordsTricky words you cannot Fred Talk, like “the”, “said” or “was”. Children learn them by sight.
Pure soundsSaying a sound short and clean: “mmm”, not “muh”. This makes blending much easier.

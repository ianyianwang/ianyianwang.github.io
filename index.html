Week 11 · Year 7

Homework spotlight: Year 7 is on a roll

  1. Everyone handed it in. This week's homework was a set of review questions on Unit 2.3, and every Year 7 student completed it. A few weeks ago, about 8 in 10 students were handing in their homework.
  2. And it's better work, too. The class scored an average of 90% on the Unit 2.3 review questions. On the Unit 2.2 review questions a few weeks ago, the average was 63%. This time, 15 students got every question right.
  3. Readers, assemble. ReadTheory is our online reading practice. In Week 9, 14 students did their reading quizzes. In Week 10, every student did, and 22 of 24 finished all four quizzes.
Week 11 · Year 9

Project spotlight: Year 9 meets the Purple Community Fund

  1. Year 9 kicked off Design Thinking Project 2. Their client this time is the Purple Community Fund (PCF), a charity that supports children and families in the Philippines.
  2. Six groups, three real challenges: corporate gifts made from ring pulls, eco-fashion and festival wear, and a catalogue of learning videos for the children at PCF, most of whom don't speak English.
  3. First stop: empathy. Who is the user, what do they need, and what bugs them about what's out there now? After that: ideas, prototypes, testing and a group pitch.

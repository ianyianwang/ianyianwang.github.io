🎃Halloween
Week 9

Halloween spotlight: the crew behind the spooky

  1. Set-up (Thursday): student volunteers in house shirts, teachers on ladders and even a few little helpers turned the school into spider territory. Tape, twine, webbing and one very large spider headband.
  2. The big day (Friday): costumes everywhere. Mr. Wang showed up as a Shopee Food rider. Yes, the box was real. No, there was no food in it.
  3. Clean-up (Friday): everything went back into neatly labelled bags ("Spiders", "Lanterns", "Webbing"), despite some unprecedented complications. Thank you, crew!
